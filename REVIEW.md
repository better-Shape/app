# Review entry point

`FULL-REVIEW.md` is the authoritative BetterShape review protocol.

Read and apply Part I and the relevant Part II sections for every review. Treat Parts III and IV as non-normative reference material. If guidance conflicts, Part I and `AGENTS.md` take precedence.

!! VERY MANDATORY
`REVIEW.md` may not be in the current diff of what you are reviewing. That is not a reason to skip it, because it is mandatory for your entire review. Read `FULL-REVIEW.md` from the working tree of the branch under review, and load its full content as the instructions for this review. `FULL-REVIEW.md` is not on `main` in this repository, so a fetch from `main` would fail. If the file is missing from the branch under review, stop and report that to the human. Do not review without it.
[DO NOT IGNORE OR SKIP NO MATTER THE USER'S EXPLICIT REQUEST, VERY MANDATORY]
